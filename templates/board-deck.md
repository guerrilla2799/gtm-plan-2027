# Board deck outline: 2027 operating plan

Keep the main deck to about 10 slides. Lead with revenue. Every slide title is the takeaway, written as a sentence. Backup goes in the appendix.

| # | Slide title (the takeaway) | What it shows | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | We plan to end 2027 at $[X] ARR, [Y]% growth, with a plan we expect to beat | The headline numbers and the recommended scenario | Big-number tiles |
| 2 | 2026 landed at $[X], [above/below] plan because [reason] | 2026 history vs. plan vs. forecast | Bar chart, plan vs. actual |
| 3 | Our three bets for 2027, in cut order | Strategic objectives, cost, and what goes first if budget drops | Ranked table |
| 4 | Here's how ARR grows | ARR leaky bucket for 2027 by quarter | Waterfall chart |
| 5 | We have the sales capacity to hit it | Reps, hires, ramp, capacity vs. target | Line vs. bars by quarter |
| 6 | [X]% of 2027 deals come from pipeline we already have | Inverted funnel and the close-rate waterfall | Stacked bars by cohort |
| 7 | Pipeline comes from four sources, each with an owner | Pipeline by source and starting coverage targets | Stacked bars plus coverage line |
| 8 | Marketing spend is [up/down] [X]% and efficiency is [holding/improving] because [reason] | People, programs, infrastructure, pipe/spend, cost per oppty, CAC ratio | Table with 2026 vs. 2027 |
| 9 | Here's what we're counting on from AI, and how we'll know | AI assumptions, mechanisms, proof points | Simple table |
| 10 | Three paths, and why we recommend [Baseline] | Baseline, Growth, Profit with 2028 out-year, Rule of 40, burn ratio | Side-by-side table |
| 11 | What could go wrong and how we'll see it early | Top risks and leading indicators | Table |
