# What to gather before you start

You can start with none of this. The skill will use labeled benchmarks for anything missing. But the plan gets much better with your own history.

## From finance
- 2026 starting and expected ending ARR
- 2026 new logo, expansion, and churn ARR by quarter
- Gross margin, and R&D plus G&A as a share of revenue
- The 2027 target or range the CEO and board have floated
- The ratios finance cares most about (CAC ratio, payback, burn multiple)

## From the CRM (RevOps)
- Oppties created by quarter for the last 6 to 8 quarters
- For each creation quarter, how many were won, and in which quarter (this gives time-based close rates)
- Average deal size by segment
- Starting pipeline at day one of each 2026 quarter, and what closed that quarter
- Pipeline by source: marketing, SDR or outbound, sales, partners

## From sales
- AEs today, ramped and ramping
- 2027 hiring plan by quarter
- Ramp time and ramped productivity from 2026 actuals
- Attrition

## From marketing
- 2026 spend by line: people, programs by channel, tools, AI tools
- Pipe/spend and cost per oppty by channel
- Self-reported attribution data, if you collect it
- Any AI pilots and their measured results
