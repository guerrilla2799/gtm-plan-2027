# AI assumptions check

Part of the 2027 Marketing Planning Skill Pack. For a full annual plan, use the `gtm-plan-2027` skill. Use this one to check the AI claims in any plan.

The core test comes from Dave Kellogg's question about whether improving ratios are real or just spreadsheet magic. See `references/sources.md`. He isn't affiliated with this skill.

## Ground rules

1. Every AI gain needs a mechanism: what work changes, who stops doing it, and how much time it frees, measured, not guessed.
2. Freed time becomes either more output or fewer hires. Never count it twice.
3. A gain with a mechanism and a mid-year proof point can go in the base plan. A gain without one is upside only.
4. AI tool spend is a real budget line. It has to be visible next to the headcount it replaces.
5. AI only works on clean data. Check data readiness before trusting any gain.

## Workflow

### 1. Collect the claims
Ask for the plan, budget, or headcount model. Find every place it counts on AI, stated or implied. Common ones: fewer SDR or content hires, agents doing inbound response or research, faster campaign production, lower agency spend, better conversion from personalization, answer engines (AEO) replacing SEO traffic.

### 2. Test each claim
Read `references/ai-era-assumptions.md`. For each claim, fill in:
- The claim, in one line
- Mechanism: what work AI does and who stops doing it
- Baseline: is today's number measured or guessed?
- The number it changes in the plan (hires, spend, conversion, pipeline)
- Double count check: output or headcount, not both
- Proof point: what you'll see, and by when (default end of Q2)
- Owner
- Verdict: Plan, Upside, or Cut

### 3. Check what's missing
- Is AI tool and usage spend in the budget, in one place?
- What new roles does the plan need (AI ops, GTM engineer, data owner)?
- Is CRM and data quality good enough for the AI work to land?
- Does the plan account for losing some classic organic search traffic to answer engines?

### 4. Deliver
1. A table of every claim with the fields above
2. The total savings in Plan vs. Upside
3. Missing items from step 3
4. A one-line "AI in this plan" summary for the board: what we're counting on, what it saves, and how we'll know

Use `references/tensions.md` if the person pushes back on moving a claim to upside.

## Style
Skeptical but fair. Plain words. Give credit to claims that hold up.
