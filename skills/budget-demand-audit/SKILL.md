# Budget and demand audit

Part of the 2027 Marketing Planning Skill Pack. For a full annual plan, use the `gtm-plan-2027` skill. Use this one to review a budget on its own, at planning time or mid-year.

The budget lens is built on Dave Kellogg's Kellblog writing on marketing budgets and board questions. See `references/sources.md`. He isn't affiliated with this skill.

## Ground rules

1. Every dollar ties to pipeline or a named objective.
2. Score channels on pipe/spend and cost per oppty first. Use self-reported attribution as a second lens, not a replacement. See `references/tensions.md`.
3. Compare to the company's own history before any benchmark. Benchmarks in `references/benchmarks.md` are fallbacks only.
4. Any ratio that's better than last year needs a reason.
5. Don't invent numbers. Label every assumption.

## Workflow

### 1. Inputs
Ask for:
- Marketing spend by line: people, programs by channel, martech, AI tools and usage
- Pipeline and oppties sourced by channel, plus win rate by channel if they have it
- Any self-reported attribution data ("How did you hear about us?")
- Lead volume by type: declared intent (asked for sales) vs. low intent (content downloads and the like)
- The pipeline target marketing owns
- Any GTM spend that sits outside the marketing budget

### 2. Three views of the budget
Build each as a table:
1. People vs. programs vs. infrastructure, with AI tools broken out. Note how the mix has moved over time.
2. Strategic programs, fixed costs, and experiments. Compare to the usual ranges in `references/plan-approval.md`.
3. Demand spend by channel with pipe/spend, cost per oppty, and win rate.

### 3. Capture vs. create
Read `references/demand-system.md`. Sort each channel into capture (in-market buyers) or create (future buyers). Show the split. Flag if creation has no budget, or if it has budget but no leading indicators.

### 4. Waste audit
Estimate how much spend goes to low-intent sources and what share of pipeline they really produce. Include the hidden cost: SDR and AE time spent on them.

### 5. Recommendations
For each channel: scale, fix, or cut, with one line of reasoning. Build a reallocation table (from, to, amount, expected pipeline impact). Check the total still covers the pipeline target at the expected pipe/spend.

### 6. Board check
Run the budget questions from `references/kellogg-planning.md`. Note any you can't answer yet.

### 7. Deliver
1. Summary: total spend, mix, pipe/spend, cost per oppty, and the one-line verdict
2. The three budget views
3. Capture vs. create split
4. Waste audit findings
5. Scale, fix, cut list and the reallocation table
6. Open questions for the board

## Style
Plain words. Lead with dollars. Be honest about channels that don't work.
