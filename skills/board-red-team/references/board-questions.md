# Board red-team questions (Phase 11)

Run every question against the plan. Score each Strong, Weak, or Missing. Fix the weak ones or list them as open risks in the plan doc.

The marketing budget questions are paraphrased from Dave Kellogg's post on board-level questions about the marketing budget (see `sources.md`). The rest add the full-GTM, approval, and AI angles.

## Growth and efficiency
1. Does the ARR bridge add up, and does it match the board's expected growth rate?
2. What's the burn ratio and Rule of 40 in each scenario?
3. Is CAC ratio or payback getting better or worse vs. 2026? Why?
4. How much of 2027 bookings is already covered by pipeline that exists today?

## Marketing budget (Kellogg)
5. How does spend split across people, programs, and infrastructure, and how has that mix moved over time?
6. Is there marketing money sitting outside the marketing budget?
7. Do the CRO and CMO agree on the budget and priorities? Where do they differ?
8. Is there a real plan for the demand gen dollars, and can it be checked against history?
9. Is marketing only chasing pipegen, or does it also own starting coverage?
10. Does the plan show pipe/spend and cost per oppty? If those ratios improve, is there a real reason?
11. How much is going to hard-to-measure work like brand? Can the CMO defend it, and do the CEO and CRO back it?
12. Can we trust this team to deliver the plan if we fund it?

## Capacity and pipeline
13. Is sales capacity enough to hit the number, with realistic ramp and attrition?
14. Does each quarter start with enough coverage, by segment?
15. Does each of the four pipeline sources have an owner and a target?

## Approval
16. Does every objective tie to a company goal?
17. Is the plan on one page, with metrics in money terms?
18. What gets cut first if the budget drops 15%?

## AI
19. What AI gains does the plan count on, and how will we know by mid-year?
20. Is AI tool spend fully visible in the budget?

## Risk
21. What are the top three ways this plan misses, and what's the early warning for each?
