# Acme Flow 2027 GTM plan on a page (fictional example)

Acme Flow is a made-up $30M ARR mid-market workflow SaaS company. Every number below comes from `gtm_plan_2027.xlsx`, built with `inputs.json` in this folder.

## The number
- Ending ARR: $40.4M (34.7% growth). New logo $9.5M, expansion $3.0M, churn and downsell $2.1M.
- Total S&M: $14.15M. CAC ratio 1.13x. Rough Rule of 40: 30.5%.
- Scenario recommended: Baseline. Growth gets to 41% but nearly triples the burn ratio (0.44x vs. 0.14x). Profit is cash-positive but slows growth to 30%.

## Strategic objectives (ranked, so the cut order is already decided)
1. Win mid-market ops teams in North America. Why: 70% of 2026 wins and our best close rates. Cost: most of the $1.5M capture budget.
2. Build demand with buyers who aren't shopping yet. Why: capture is capped by in-market buyers. Cost: $0.8M creation budget.
3. Prove AI-first inbound response. Why: cuts SDR hiring if it works. Cost: $250K AI tools. This is first to cut.

## How we get there
- Capacity: 20 AEs at year end, 10 hires, 2-quarter ramp. Capacity covers 1.30x of the new logo target.
- Pipeline: 794 new oppties ($35.7M pipeline). 42% of 2027 deals (88 of 211) come from oppties that already exist.
- Sources: marketing 50%, SDR/outbound 25%, sales 15%, partners 10%.
- Coverage: start every quarter at 3.0x of that quarter's new logo target ($5.4M on day one of Q1, $9.0M for Q4).
- Demand: capture $1.5M at 12:1 pipe/spend, create $0.8M, experiments $0.5M.

## AI in this plan
- We're counting on: an AI agent for first-touch inbound response and research. It saves 2 SDR hires. We'll know by end of Q2 because speed-to-lead and meeting conversion will match or beat the human baseline. If not, we hire the SDRs and the saving moves to upside.

## Top 3 risks and early warnings
1. Back-half deals lag target. The waterfall shows H1 ahead and Q4 about $445K behind. Warning sign: Q4 starting pipeline under $9.0M by week 8 of Q3.
2. Hiring slips. Q1 and Q2 hires drive H2 capacity. Warning sign: fewer than 6 AEs signed by end of Q1.
3. Pipe/spend drops below 12:1. Warning sign: two months in a row below 10:1 on capture spend.

## Where we see it differently
- Marketing is about half of S&M, which is high for our stage. We're keeping it because 50% of pipeline is marketing-sourced and pipe/spend has held. If the ratio slips, we'll move money to outbound, not cut the creation budget first.
- We score channels on pipe/spend (Kellogg) and also track self-reported attribution. When they disagree, we show both.
